Film Screenings

Showcasing independent films from emerging creators.

Indie Film Collective brings people together through the power of independent cinema by showcasing films from emerging filmmakers, creative storytellers, and independent artists. From short films and documentaries to experimental projects, our screenings highlight unique perspectives, diverse experiences, and original storytelling. We aim to create a welcoming space where film lovers can discover new talent, explore different ideas, and connect with others who share a passion for cinema. Through special screenings, filmmaker discussions, and community events, we celebrate creativity and give independent artists an opportunity to share their work with a wider audience.
